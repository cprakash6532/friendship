# friendship
Friendship is one of the most beautiful relationships in life. A true friend is someone who always supports us, understands us, and stands by our side in both happy and difficult times. Friends share our joys, sorrows, secrets, and memories. They make our lives happier and more meaningful. 
## Friendship

Friendship is one of the most precious gifts in our life. It is a special bond between two or more people built on love, trust, care, honesty, and understanding. A true friend is like a family member who makes us smile when we are sad and supports us when we face difficulties. Friends are the people with whom we can share our happiness, sadness, secrets, dreams, and feelings without fear. They make every moment beautiful and every memory unforgettable. A good friend never leaves us alone during difficult times and always encourages us to do better in life. Friendship teaches us important values such as sharing, helping, respecting, forgiving, and caring for others. We may have many friends, but a true friend is someone who accepts us for who we are, including our weaknesses and mistakes. Sometimes, even a small conversation with a friend can make our whole day better. Friends fight sometimes, but true friendship is strong enough to overcome misunderstandings. Distance and time may separate friends, but they can never break a genuine bond. A true friend is not someone who is with us only during good times but someone who stays beside us during the worst moments. Life becomes more colourful, joyful, and meaningful with good friends. A true friend is not just a part of our life; they become a beautiful part of our heart. ❤️
class Friendship:
    def __init__(self, friend):
        self.friend = friend

    def happiness(self):
        print("Friends make life beautiful! ❤️")

    def support(self):
        print("A true friend always supports you.")

    def forever(self):
        print("True friendship lasts forever! 🫂")


my_friendship = Friendship("Best Friend")

my_friendship.happiness()
my_friendship.support()
my_friendship.forever()
